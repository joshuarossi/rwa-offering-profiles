Real-World Asset Offering Profiles

Demonstration offering profiles for tokenized real-world assets.

ACRE · Harvest Acres Farmland

Fractional ownership of 1,240 acres of prime Iowa row-crop farmland.

AURV · Aurum Vault Gold

One token. One troy ounce of allocated, LBMA Good Delivery gold.

CRU · Cru Reserve Fine Wine

A curated cellar of investment-grade Bordeaux and Burgundy, stored in bond.

HELX · Helios Solar Yield

Revenue participation in a 48 MW operating solar park in Andalusia, Spain.

TBLL · Meridian Treasury Bill Fund

Tokenized units of a short-duration U.S. Treasury bill portfolio.
